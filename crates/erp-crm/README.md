# erp-crm

Lead intake pipelines, deal stages, communication tracking, and weighted opportunity conversion scoring.

---

## 📦 Overview

`erp-crm` organizes lead acquisition, qualification workflows, and sales pipelines.

### Key Capabilities

- **Pipeline Management (`pipeline.rs`)**: Lead stages, opportunity progression, and expected revenue metrics.
- **Lead Scoring (`scoring.rs`)**: Rule-based qualification scores determining hot/warm leads for sales prioritization.
