# erp-support

Support ticket lifecycles, Service Level Agreements (SLA), escalation matrices, and response gameplans.

---

## 📦 Overview

`erp-support` manages incoming customer issues, SLA resolution deadlines, and escalation hierarchies.

### Key Capabilities

- **SLA Management (`sla.rs`)**: Configurable first-response and resolution SLA timers by customer tier.
- **Gameplans (`gameplan.rs`)**: Structured triage playbooks and action steps for support agents.
